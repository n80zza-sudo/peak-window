Peak Gains privacy policy

Peak Gains is a personal workout reminder app.

What we collect

Nothing is sent to us. Your levels, settings, drills, name and workout history are stored only on your device. The app has no accounts, analytics, ads or servers.

Permissions

The app asks for notification permission, and on Android for exact alarms, only to show your workout reminders.

Your data

You can export a backup from Settings, and deleting the app removes all of its data.
